And the suburb pages are **not** lazy geo-swaps — each has its own `introHeading`, `intro`,

exception."* / Solon: *"We come to you. You don't move a thing."* / Pepper Pike: *"You have nice

**So the bottleneck is not a content gap. Google has discovered these pages and is declining to
spend crawl budget indexing them.** "Discovered – currently not indexed" at this scale is the
classic low-authority / low-corroboration signal for a young entity.

> **Writing more pages right now would be actively counterproductive** — it adds to a pile of 14
> pages Google already won't index. This is exactly the ZEIROS thesis: *entity trust is step zero;
> rankings pay authority, not merit.*

**Caveat to verify:** city-page reviews are reused across suburbs (Deborah + Mark Fisher appear on
both Beachwood and Pepper Pike; Mark Michaels on both Solon and Pepper Pike). If those are real
reviews attributed to towns they didn't come from, fix it — it's both an integrity and a quality

**Nearly all impressions are branded.** Non-branded discovery queries ("…near me", "car detailing
mayfield") get impressions and **zero clicks** at position ~10.

### This settles the "east side" question with data

Nobody searches *"east side."* Real searchers use **"near me"** and **suburb names**
("car detailing **mayfield**"). FMD ranks #1 for "east side" precisely because **it is the only
business using the phrase** — a #1 ranking on a zero-volume term is worth nothing. Lou's instinct
was right on both counts, and the data agrees:

- ❌ "Cleveland's East Side" — zero search volume, and carries the East Cleveland connotation.
- ✅ Lead with **named suburbs** (Beachwood, Solon, Pepper Pike, Mayfield Heights) — matches actual
  query behavior, avoids the connotation, and is more specific and credible.
- ✅ Where a regional label is unavoidable, **"Cleveland's eastern suburbs"** reads as affluent;
  *"east side"* reads as urban core.

| Interaction | Count |
|---|---|

| Website clicks | 17 |
| Calls | 4 |

**Top search terms surfacing the profile:** "fresh car detailing", "fresh mobile detailing",
**"mobile rv wash near cleveland, oh"**.

### Two GBP problems worth real money

1. **29 of 50 interactions are direction requests — to a mobile business, at a residential
   address.** Nobody should be driving to 6605 Kingswood Dr; Fresh goes to *them*. This is the
   signature of a profile configured as a storefront rather than a **Service Area Business**.
   Hiding the address and setting the service area should convert that misdirected intent into
   calls/clicks/bookings — and stops publishing a home address.
2. **Category bleed.** `Car repair and maintenance service` and `Auto upholsterer` are almost
   certainly what surfaced *"mobile rv wash near cleveland"*. They pull irrelevant queries and
   dilute the relevance of the one category that matters. `Car detailing service` is correct;
   consider dropping or replacing the other two (`Car wash` is a closer adjacency than `Car
   repair`).
3. **0 bookings despite a live Square booking link** — worth checking the link actually works.
