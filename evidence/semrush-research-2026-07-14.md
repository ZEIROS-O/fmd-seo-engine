## 4. The structural finding: homepages win city terms — suburb pages don't, even with 10× our authority

Where each competitor's Cleveland rankings actually live:

- **Nathan's** (254 ref domains): homepage ranks **#1** "mobile detailing cleveland ohio",
  **#1** "mobile car detailing cleveland ohio", **#3** "auto detailing cleveland", **#4** "car
  detailing cleveland". Their dedicated pages — `/cleveland-car-detail/`,
  `/cleveland-heights-car-detail/`, `/euclid-car-detail/`, `/mobile-car-detailing/` — rank
  **36–96** for the same terms. The homepage cannibalizes every city page they built.
- **ATF** (162 ref domains): every ranking is the homepage — #5 "mobile auto detailing
  cleveland", #7 "mobile car detailing cleveland ohio", #9 "mobile car detailing cleveland",
  #11 "auto detailing cleveland ohio", #14 "auto/detail terms". Zero interior pages ranking.
- **CeramicPro CLE** (791 ref domains): homepage #9–10 on the Cleveland cluster; their suburb
  pages (`/car-detailing/solon/`, `/car-detailing/aurora/`) manage only #18–23 *in Aurora*, a
  town with its own volume.

**This is third-party confirmation of the baseline conclusion.** Competitors with 2–7× our
referring domains and years of history still can't rank suburb pages — Google routes local
service intent to homepages + map pack. Our 9 unindexed `/areas` pages aren't underperforming;
they're behaving exactly like everyone else's.
