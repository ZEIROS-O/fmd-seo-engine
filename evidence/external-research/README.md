# evidence/external-research/

Third-party studies and industry findings that inform the thesis but are **not**
measurements of FMD.

Rules for anything filed here:

- The primary source and its reliability are recorded in a `provenance` block.
  If we only hold a secondhand summary, say so and say who wrote it and what
  they were selling.
- Numbers from here **never** enter `brain/state.yaml` or `brain/thesis.md` as
  measured facts. They are priors and hypothesis generators. `brain/state.yaml`
  values trace to an FMD measurement, always (GATE-4).
- The author's inferences are kept separate from the study's numbers.
